Understanding & Calculating APS

Your Admission Point Score (APS) converts your NSC matric percentages into points (1 to 7) to determine eligibility for higher education programmes. Most South African universities calculate APS using your best six subjects excluding Life Orientation.

  • Level 7 (80–100%): 7 points
  • Level 6 (70–79%): 6 points
  • Level 5 (60–69%): 5 points
  • Level 4 (50–59%): 4 points
  • Level 3 (40–49%): 3 points

Multiple Routes to Success

Pathways Beyond University

TVET Colleges

National Certificate Vocational (NCV) and Report 191 (NATED) programmes combining theory with practical workplace training.

Learnerships & Apprenticeships

SETA-accredited structured learning programmes where you earn a monthly stipend while gaining an NQF qualification or trade test.

Skills Programmes & Entrepreneurship

Short accredited digital, technical, and business incubators designed to launch micro-enterprises or entry-level careers.
