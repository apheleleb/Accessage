We Listen Before We Lead

Learner Voices Initiative

Placing South African learners at the center of educational research, career guidance design, and youth advocacy.

Purpose

Why Learner Voice Matters

Policies and career programmes often fail because learners are rarely asked what support they actually need in their schools and communities.

Methodology

How We Gather Insights

We combine provincial digital surveys, school engagement workshops, and youth-led focus groups to capture honest perspectives.

Reports

Learner Voices Reports

Findings are synthesized into open-access briefs shared with educators, youth organizations, and partners.

Participate

Take the Learner Survey

Your responses are anonymous and directly inform the career resources, bursary guides, and workshops ACCESSAGE creates next.

What We Ask

  • Current Grade & Province
  • School Type & Access to Career Guidance
  • Career Interests & Post-School Plans
  • Biggest Challenges & Opportunity Interests
