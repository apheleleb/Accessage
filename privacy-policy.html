Privacy Policy

How ACCESSAGE collects, protects, and respects your personal information in compliance with South African standards (POPIA).

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

ACCESSAGE ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of personal information collected from learners, educators, volunteers, and partners across South Africa. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and protection under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you participate in our Learner Voices survey, submit an opportunity, apply as a volunteer, or contact our team. This may include:

  • Contact details (such as name and email address)
  • Educational background (grade, province, school type, career interests)
  • Responses to surveys and research feedback
  • Enquiry messages and collaboration requests

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To develop career guidance resources, bursary guides, and educational content
  • To compile aggregate, anonymized Learner Voices research reports
  • To respond to enquiries, partnership proposals, and volunteer applications
  • To send newsletter updates and opportunity alerts (only if opted in)

4. Data Protection & Consent

We adhere strictly to our core principle: We listen before we lead. We seek consent before we share. We put learners first. Personal data is stored securely, treated with strict confidentiality, and never sold or shared with commercial third parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at:
